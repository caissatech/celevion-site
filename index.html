Small offline solo mobile games, made in France.

Celevion is an independent studio founded by Loïc Cantin. We build compact, single-player games that live entirely on your phone: no account, no server, no connection once the game is installed.

What we make

Short, complete sessions
Games built around one clear idea, playable in a few minutes and satisfying to finish.
Played fully offline
No network needed to play, no backend to run, nothing to sign up for.
On your own
Single-player, designed to be understood without a tutorial wall or a manual.

The studio

Founded in 2026 and based in France, Celevion handles design, programming, artwork and publishing in-house, with no publisher in between. Our first title is in development for Android, on Google Play.

We build for players who want a small game they can install, understand in a minute and keep, not a service that asks for their attention every day.

Privacy

This site is plain HTML and CSS: no JavaScript, no external fonts, no analytics, no cookies, no third-party content of any kind. Nothing about your visit is recorded here, and nothing leaves your browser.
